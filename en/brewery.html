BREWERY

Cauldron fermentation, alembic distillation, and barrel cellar aging

1
Brewing Methodology

1. Fermentation: Fill a cauldron over heat with water. Right-click required ingredients inside. Boil for the recipe duration and draw with glass bottles.
2. Distillation: Place the brew inside a Brewing Stand, and insert glowstone dust into the top slot as a purifying filter.
3. Aging: Build an aging barrel using the specified wood. Rest the drink inside. 1 barrel year = 20 real minutes.
4. Sealing: To bottle commercially, craft a Sealing Table (2 glass bottles + 4 wooden planks) to conceal the recipe and age from competitors.
