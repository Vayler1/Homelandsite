Evaluation & Masterpiece Status

• Appraisal: Each monument is assessed by material coefficient. Decorative details (lanterns, benches, paintings) award +1–2 bonus points.
• Revenue: Adaptive daily municipal dividend paid for every 1,000 value points of your structure.
• Masterpieces: If 80% of staff architects rate the monument as a "Masterpiece", you receive +20% score value OR a unique server buff for all city residents!
• Limited Server Wonders: Can be erected anywhere (strictly 1 instance per server). Yield 100 coins per 1,000 points plus an active buff, but cannot claim Masterpiece status.
