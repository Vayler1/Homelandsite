ANARCHY ZONE: THE MOON

Zero-gravity warfare, heavy artillery, and outpost supremacy

EARTH LAWS CEASE TO EXIST HERE! Griefing, unprovoked murder, and TNT artillery are completely legal. Teleportation commands /sethome, /tpa, and /tpahere are strictly disabled.
1
Access & Lunar Gravity

A space shuttle stands ready at server spawn. Climb the interior ladder and right-click the "Launch" sign. On the Moon, low gravity physics govern movement: leaps carry you high into the sky and descents are slow and floating.

2
Outpost Battles & Meteoric Gold

Storming the Outpost

Reach the outpost banner (it must remain clear of blocks) and run /forpost attack. Accumulate 100 control points within 10 minutes to seize control.

Hourly Dividends

Every hour, controlled outposts generate cash dividends and rare Meteoric Gold directly into your nation treasury. Cap: 3 outposts per nation/alliance.

Schedules & Cooldowns

Attacks are permitted from 12:00 to 22:00. Standard capture cooldown is 1 hour. After 20:30, the cooldown vanishes for nonstop battles!

Allied Trusts

Outpost commanders can whitelist trusted allies: /forpost trust add [town] [nick]. If an outpost is conquered, all existing trust permissions reset.
