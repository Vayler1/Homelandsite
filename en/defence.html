Interceptions, Concussion & Anti-Grief Protection

Each UAV homes in on town coordinates or dives toward nearby players:

Marksmanship

🏹 Intercepting Targets

A UAV is destroyed with 1 precise shot from a bow, crossbow, or trident. Wreckage drops valuable scrap: iron, copper, and redstone dust.

Detonation

💥 Player Concussion

Unintercepted drones crash and explode. Direct impacts are lethal. The shockwave inflicts severe concussion — blindness and slowness.

Asset Protection

🛡️ Anti-Grief Safeguards (Blocks Immune to Explosions)

Player assets are strictly guarded against destruction. Drone explosions will NEVER break:

  • ❌ Chests, double chests, barrels
  • ❌ Shulkers, furnaces, anvils
  • ❌ Ender chests and mob spawners
  • ❌ Enchanting tables and beacons
  • ❌ Diamond, netherite, emerald blocks
  • ❌ Gold, iron blocks, and obsidian
3
Energy Crisis: Municipal Blackouts

If a drone detonates inside municipal borders, there is a 50% chance of grid damage. Power outages dynamically last between 1 and 5 real hours.

💡 Lighting Failure

All artificial municipal illumination fails: street lanterns, torches, and redstone lamps go dark.

🔌 Redstone Disruption

Electrical logic is paralyzed: buttons and levers cannot be toggled, and redstone circuits lock up.

🌫️ Pitch Darkness & Fog

At night, a heavy suffocating dark fog descends upon residents, escalating mob ambush hazards.
