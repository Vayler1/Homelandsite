TOWNS & INFRASTRUCTURE

Municipal administration, logistics networks, quarries & tourism hubs

1
Municipal Basics & Road Hierarchy

• City Hall: The foundational building of any settlement (min 2 chunks, free). Must house mayor & deputy offices, plus a reception hall. Logistics must hook into it.
• Logistics Center: Connects all municipal industry (min 4 chunks). Without it, manufacturing cannot run! Requires: manager office, warehouse, sorting bay, and loading docks.

Tier 1

Trail / Dirt Path

From 3 blocks wide. Free building connectivity.

Tier 2

Cobble / Stone Road

From 5 blocks wide (andesite, diorite, stone, deepslate, brick). 100 coins hookup fee.

Tier 3

Asphalt Highway

From 9 blocks wide (concrete or blackstone) with lane markings. 300 coins hookup fee.

2
Mining Quarries (Max 1 of Each Type Per Town)

Extract ores every 24 hours based on level. Upgrades: Tier 1 to 2 costs 3,000 coins; Tier 2 to 3 costs 7,000 coins. All quarries require an administrative building with storage, mess hall, and restrooms.

Road Tier 2

Coal Quarry

Size: from 8 chunks, pit/tunnels 20+ blocks deep.
Yield: 2 / 4 / 6 stacks per 24 hours.
Cost: 2,500 coins.

Road Tier 2

Iron Mine

Size: from 10 chunks, pit 25+ blocks deep.
Yield: 1 / 2 / 3 stacks per 24 hours.
Cost: 5,000 coins.

Road Tier 3

Gold Mine

Size: from 6 chunks, pit 30+ blocks deep.
Yield: 32 / 64 / 96 raw ore per 24 hours.
Cost: 8,000 coins.

Road Tier 3

Diamond Quarry

Size: from 12 chunks, shaft reaching -40 Y.
Yield: 8 / 16 / 32 diamonds per 24 hours.
Cost: 11,000 coins.

3
Industrial Facilities & Economics
Road Tier 2

Blast Furnace (4,000)

From 4 chunks. Must contain 2 conveyor belts, furnace, and warehouse. Mass smelts resources with bonus drops.

Road Tier 2

Sawmill (1,000)

From 4 chunks. Unlocks a lumberjack NPC offering high-rate timber trades. Requires an office and log yard.

Road Tier 2

Sugar Refinery (4,000)

From 3 chunks. Yields 3 sugar per sugar cane stalk. Requires boiling room and director office.

Road Tier 1

Windmill (1,000)

At least 30 blocks tall. Mill grinds 2 wheat into 1 bread. Throughput cap: 2 stacks per hour.

Road Tier 1

Bakery (1,000)

From 2 chunks. Kitchen & dining hall. Automatically sells bread to NPC buyers (up to 128 loaves/hr).

Road Tier 3

Chocolate Factory (4,000)

From 3 chunks. Manufactures chocolate. Selling requires a "Chocolate Store" (1,500 coins, Tier 1 Road).

Transit

Bus Terminal (2,000)

From 4 chunks. Instant travel between connected cities. Mayor sets ticket pricing (up to 1,000) via /hbus setprice or declares embargo with /hbus forbid.

4
Tourism & Civic Landmarks

Racetrack (Max 1)

From 12 chunks. Speed circuit. Requires 4 pit-lanes, 2 grandstands, control tower, and paved tarmac. Commission: 2,000 coins.

Shopping Mall (Max 1)

From 16 chunks. Minimum 6 retail stores, 3 food court stalls, panoramic atrium, and parking. Commission: 6,500 coins.

Amusement Park (Max 2)

From 15 chunks. 3+ rides, 2 snack stands, 30% greenery buffer zone. Commission: 8,000 coins.

Hotel (Max 4)

From 4 chunks. Minimum 5 furnished rooms (bed, desk, chair) and reception lobby. Commission: 1,500 coins.

Ferris Wheel (Max 2)

From 4 chunks. Grand steel wheel with 8+ passenger gondolas and night illumination. Commission: 3,500 coins.

Museum (Max 3)

From 6 chunks. Monumental building with 3 thematic exhibition galleries for relics. Commission: 4,000 coins.

River Port (Max 1)

From 10 chunks along coast. Docks for 2 ships, cranes, harbor master tower, warehouses. Commission: 20,000 coins.

TV/Radio Tower (Max 1)

From 4 chunks. Skyline landmark (height 65+ blocks). Server hub, archives, warning beacon. Commission: 5,000 coins.

Parks (Max 3) & Statues (Max 5)

Urban parks from 8 chunks (70% greenery). Statues from 1 chunk built of fine materials (copper, quartz, gold) with dedication plaques.
