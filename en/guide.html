GUIDE & WAYS TO EARN

From your first teleport to building an economic empire on Homeland

1
First Steps (Quick Start)

Once you spawn in, follow these essential steps to set up your journey:

Step 1: Teleport

🌍 Enter the World

Run the /rtp command. A menu with real Ukrainian regions will open (Galicia, Volyn, Center, Slobozhanshchyna, etc.). Pick one to warp there randomly!

Step 2: Find a Home

🏘️ Join a Town

Never build alone! Run /search — online mayors will see your request and send invitations. Accept and use /t spawn.

Own Nation

🏛️ Found a Town

Found an unclaimed area? Open the map in /menu and create your settlement with /t new [name] (costs 100–200 coins).

24/7 Support

🤖 In-Game AI Assistant

Got a question about mechanics? Ask the server AI assistant directly in chat:
/ask [your question]

2
Starting Capital (Early Cash Flow)

The simplest and most reliable ways to make money without initial investment:

Steady Income

💼 Professions (/jobs)

Get employed with /jobs. Earn coins for every chopped log, mined ore, harvested crop, or caught fish.

High Margin

🔄 Hourly Merchant (/seller)

A dynamic trader at /seller. Every 60 minutes, he cycles requested goods and offers inflated rates for specific items. Keep an eye on his list!

Milestones

📜 BattlePass & Quests (/bp)

Type /bp to complete daily challenges and tier quests. Each completed tier yields significant cash and rare supplies.

Commerce

⚖️ Player Auction (/ah)

Trade with players on the auction house via /ah. Sell ores, gear, enchants, or flip underpriced listings for profit.

Spawn

🏪 Spawn NPC Markets

Direct server commerce! Sell raw commodities to spawn vendor NPCs for quick liquidation, or purchase construction materials and tools.

3
Industrial Wealth (Mines, Mills & Crafting)

Once you establish a town, scale into automated industrial production:

⛏️ Town Mining Quarries

Build city mines. They generate clean ores into storage chests every 24 hours: coal (up to 6 stacks), iron (up to 3 stacks), gold (up to 96), and diamonds (up to 32/day)!

🍞 Bread Industry (Windmill + Bakery)

The Windmill grinds 2 wheat into 1 bread (up to 2 stacks/hr), and the Bakery auto-sells up to 128 loaves per hour to NPC buyers for steady cash.

🍫 Chocolate Factory

Produce batches of fine chocolate, which can be continuously sold to specialized local merchants via the Chocolate Store building.

🍺 Artisan Brewery (Brewery)

Brew aged luxury alcohols (Whiskey, Rum, "Moskal's Blood"). Seal bottles at the Sealing Table to hide recipes and sell them as buff-granting delicacies.

🪵 Lumber Processing

The Sawmill unlocks a specialized lumberjack NPC with whom you can trade processed wood products at elevated market rates.

🚌 Intercity Bus Network

Build a Bus Station and set passenger transit ticket fees up to 1000 coins (/hbus setprice). Travel fares go directly into your town treasury!

4
Passive Income, Tourism & Landmarks
Passive Cash

🏛️ Grand Landmarks

Construct historical regional monuments (10+ chunks). Cities receive daily payouts for every 1000 material valuation points. Masterpieces yield +20% bonus revenue!

Exclusive

⭐ Limited Wonders

Unique server-wide monuments (limit 1 on the whole server). Yield elevated dividends: 100 coins per 1000 score points daily into the town vault.

Tourism

🎡 Malls, Parks & Hotels

Shopping Malls (16+ chunks), water amusement parks, speedway racetracks, and hotels raise city prestige, generate revenue, and attract tax-paying citizens.

Priesthood

⛪ Clergy Services

Erect a consecrated Church and get ordained as Priest. Charge official fees for conducting marriage ceremonies and divorce proceedings via /pay.

5
High-Risk Ventures (High Risk — Massive Payoff)
Heists

🏴‍☠️ Nation Bank Vault Raids

Between 18:00 and 20:00, initiate a raid on an enemy nation town with /homelandwar raid. Hold ground for 10 minutes to loot 5% of the entire enemy nation bank!

Outerspace

🌙 Lunar Outposts

Conquer lunar bases using /forpost attack. Controlled outposts yield hourly cash dividends and supply rare Meteoric Gold.

Hunting

🩸 Blood Night Farming

Every 12 days, hunt mutated horde mobs that drop raw diamonds, gold, and Netherite Scraps, while the Warden raid boss drops a Nether Star.

Subsidies

🛡️ City Rebuild Fund

Every enemy drone strike that lands inside your borders is compensated by the state! Mayors collect 100 to 500 coins per hit via /rebuildfunds.
