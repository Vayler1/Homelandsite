NATION MILITARY DOCTRINE

Geopolitical wars, supremacy campaigns, bank vault heists & War Score

MARTIAL LAW FINANCIAL LOCKDOWN: During an active war, withdrawing money from town or nation banks is prohibited (deposits only). Leaving a nation costs 20,000 coins, and disbanding a nation during war is strictly banned!
1
Declaring War, 48h Grace Period & 24/7 PvP

Wars are declared exclusively between nations (independent towns without a nation cannot engage in formal wars). There is no minimum member threshold, and nations can fight multi-front wars concurrently.

Initiation

📢 Casus Belli

War can only be declared by the Nation President via:
/homelandwar attack <nation>

Grace Period

🛡️ 48-Hour Immunity

Newly founded nations enjoy 48 hours of protection. However, if a young nation initiates an attack, its shield instantly expires.

Combat Rules

⚔️ 24/7 Mutual PvP

PvP activates across all territories of both belligerents 24/7. Damage can only be exchanged by combatants. Third-party interference is blocked!

Diplomacy

🌐 Front Independence

If Nation A fights B and C, while D fights B — every distinct war theater tracks an isolated score independent of external conflicts.

2
War Score & Automatic Capitulation

Wars cannot drag on infinitely in the face of decisive battlefield superiority. The current War Score is displayed on DynMap next to nation titles:

+10 War Score

Awarded for eliminating an ordinary citizen or soldier of the hostile nation.

High Value Target

+50 War Score

Awarded for assassinating an enemy Town Mayor or Nation President.

City Siege

+100 War Score

Awarded for completing a successful bank heist and looting an enemy settlement.

Victory

🏆 1,000 Points = Total Triumph

Upon reaching 1,000 points, the opposing state suffers unconditional defeat and automatically capitulates!

3
Town Raids (State Vault Heists)

If enemies retreat to fortified bases and avoid open ground skirmishes, you can strike their central economic vaults directly:

⏰ Heist Window

Raids can only be launched from 18:00 to 20:00 Kyiv time. Restricted to 1 raid per 24 hours against the same nation.

⚔️ Siege Protocol

Infiltrators enter the hostile town and trigger:
/homelandwar raid
The assault lasts 10 minutes. At least one attacker must hold ground inside the borders continuously.

War Spoils

💰 Successful Heist Bounty

• Invading nation claims +100 War Score.
• 5% of the defending NATION'S treasury is siphoned into the attackers' state budget!

🛡️ Defense Conditions

If defenders eliminate all raiders or repel them outside town borders before the 10-minute timer expires, the raid completely fails.

4
War Maintenance Tax (Economic Cost of War)

War drains economic stability. Remaining in a state of active war levies a daily military upkeep tax:

📊 Tax Formula

Total nation citizens × 300 coins.
For every citizen, the nation pays 300 coins daily during Towny bank clearing.

🏛️ Treasury Deduction

The tax is deducted directly from the NATION treasury (shared state account). DynMap displays total nation war upkeep.

⚠️ Withdrawal Ban

Throughout the duration of war, withdrawing funds with `/t withdraw` is locked. Only deposits (`/t deposit`) are permitted.

War Resolution: Treaties & Capitulation
Diplomacy

✍️ Bilateral Peace Treaty

Wars do not expire automatically. Ending hostilities by mutual consent requires mutual confirmation:

Step 1 (Proposer):
/homelandwar peace <nation>

Step 2 (Acceptor):
/homelandwar confirmpeace <nation>

PvP between the two nations ceases; independent wars with other states continue.

Defeat

🏳️ Capitulation & Reparations

Unconditional surrender declared by the defeated President:
/homelandwar capitulate <winner>

  • All active wars end immediately, disabling PvP.
  • 20% Reparations: The surrendered city pays 20% of its treasury daily for 7 days to the victor nation.
  • Secession Lock: Member towns cannot leave the victor nation for 7 days.
