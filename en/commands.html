Church, Priests & Marriage

A Town Mayor can build a consecrated regional Church and ordain a Priest (ordination fee: 5,000 coins).
• /marry [player1] [player2] — Priest solemnizes marriage (costs 2,500 coins per partner).
• /marry sethome — Establish joint marital home (12,500 coins).
• /marry tp — Teleport to spouse (5,000 coins).
• /marry gift — Gift held item to partner (300 coins).
• /marry divorce — Priest grants divorce (costs 500 coins per party).

Nation Tax Tiers

Base town upkeep is 10 coins per chunk. As your nation grows in population, dynamic tax brackets apply:

Settlement (0+ players)

Base Tax: 1,000 coins.

Nation (10+ players)

Tax: 2,000 (+10% to town upkeep)

Duchy (20+ players)

Tax: 3,000 (+20% to town upkeep)

Kingdom (30+ players)

Tax: 8,000 (+30% to town upkeep)

Empire (40+ players)

Tax: 15,000 (+40% to town upkeep)

Great Empire (60+ players)

Tax: 20,000 (+50% to town upkeep)

World Power (100+ players)

Tax: 30,000 (+60% to town upkeep)

Galactic Empire (150+ players)

Tax: 40,000 (+70% to town upkeep)

Complete Towny Command Reference

Daily town upkeep is 40 coins (must remain in the town bank). The initial chunk claim costs 40 coins; each subsequent claim price increases by 0.5% (capped at 500 coins).

/t new [name]

Create a new town (200 coins).

/t claim / unclaim

Claim or release adjacent chunk.

/t deposit [amount]

Deposit coins into town bank.

/t withdraw [amount]

Withdraw coins (Mayor only).

/t invite / kick [nick]

Invite or expel citizens.

/t set taxes [amount]

Set daily citizen poll tax.

/t spawn

Teleport to town spawnpoint.

/t set homeblock & /t set spawn

Relocate town spawn.

/plot forsale [price]

Put plot on sale for citizens.

/plot claim / unclaim

Purchase or surrender plot.

/t toggle pvp / fire / explosions

Toggle PvP, fire spread, explosions.

/t trust add [nick]

Grant trusted resident status.

Bottling Experience into Flasks

Convert your accumulated player XP into tradeable Bottles o' Enchanting! Hold empty glass bottles, approach an active Beacon, and Right-Click it. Your experience will bottle automatically with a minor conversion commission.
