BLOOD NIGHT

The ultimate survival trial: blood moon, mutated hordes, and merciless bosses

ATTENTION: GLOBAL PVP ACTIVATED! During Blood Night, unrestricted open PvP is forced across ALL towns older than 7 days. Flight command /tfly is strictly disabled. Casualties during this event do not constitute griefing!
1
Schedule, Siren Alarm & Rookie Protection

• Interval: Blood Night begins automatically every 12 in-game days.
• Server Broadcast: Minutes prior to dusk, an ominous crimson warning broadcasts to all players:
[Server]: The Blood Night is upon us! Prepare for the slaughter!
• Rookie Immunity: Players on the server for under 2 real days will not be targeted by aggressive horde swarms (though nearby standard mobs still gain mutated stats).

2
Mob Mutations & Dangerous Afflictions

Monster spawn rates increase by 200–300%, and their health pool expands by 50–100%. Regular melee hits inflict lethal damage and apply the Wither debuff.

Mutation

🧟 Zombies

Endowed with permanent Speed and Strength. Sprint down running players and crush armor plates.

Mutation

🏹 Skeletons

Enter Rapid Fire mode. Fire relentless barrages of deadly arrows with virtually zero draw cooldown.

Mutation

💥 Creepers

Detonation radius multiplied several times. A single stealth explosion can wipe out an entire armed squad.

Trait

🐛 Death Spawns (Endermites)

Killing any monster instantly spawns a swarm of ravenous Endermites from its corpse to drag out combat.

3
"The Mighty" (Potuzhny) — Blood Night Raid Boss

At midnight, Potuzhny spawns near a randomly selected player (strictly 1 Potuzhny exists per Blood Night event).

⚔️ Evoker Convocations

Every 30 seconds, Potuzhny summons a retinue of Evokers who assail warriors with fangs and Vexes.

⏳ Dawn Timer

If hunters fail to defeat Potuzhny before sunrise, he dissolves into ashes without granting any loot.

Legendary Drop

🎁 Elimination Bounty

Slaying Potuzhny drops: a Nether Star OR 3 Strength II Potions, alongside massively amplified drop multipliers!

4
Unique Drops & Resource Wealth

Despite extreme survival stakes, the Blood Night serves as the ultimate economic windfall for battle-tested warriors:

Apex Loot

🔥 Netherite Scraps

Ordinarily restricted to the Nether depths. During Blood Night, mutated surface mobs drop pure Netherite scraps.

Precious Ores

💎 Raw Diamonds & Gold

Significant chances to obtain pristine diamonds, gold ingots, and refined iron directly from slain horde creatures.

Resurrection

🗿 Totems of Undying

Obtain rare Totems of Undying to cheat death during subsequent wars, dungeon raids, and dangerous journeys!
